# Business Problem & Objectives: Contribution Profitability

## 1. The Core Merchant Dilemma
E-commerce merchants overwhelmingly manage their business by top-line gross merchandise value (GMV) or naive gross profit. GMV is not cash collected, and naive gross margin ignores the operational drag between checkout and bank deposit:
- **Order-Level & Code Discounts:** Erase margin before fulfillment.
- **Shipping Subsidies:** Free shipping promotions frequently exceed entire product gross margins on bulky or low-price goods.
- **Payment Processing Fees:** Take 2.4% - 3.5% + $0.30 per transaction, and non-Shopify-Payments gateways often mask their fees.
- **Refunds & Reverse Logistics:** Return shipping, restocking fees, and lost merchandise turn profitable sales into severe cash drains.

When merchant stores scale, profitable orders cross-subsidize money-losing orders, giving an illusion of healthy growth while draining cash reserves.

## 2. Objective of Formula F11 v2.1
Formula F11 computes exact **Contribution Profit (P)** on every order:
1. **Identifies exact loss-makers:** Isolates unprofitable orders, channels, and product categories.
2. **Decomposes profit drivers:** Attributes profit and losses to list product margin, discounts, shipping subsidy, fees, and refunds.
3. **Evidentiary integrity (No Confident Wrong Numbers):** Categorizes every order into strictly separated evidentiary lanes (`MEASURED`, `ESTIMATED`, `CONFIRMED_LOSS`, `UNDETERMINED`, `EXCLUDED`), ensuring merchants never mistake an estimate for measured bank truth.
